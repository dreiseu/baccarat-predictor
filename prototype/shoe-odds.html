How to read this

  • The best bet is the one that loses the least on average. It still loses about 49% of the time.
  • Streaks and road patterns don't change the odds. In a 3.2 million hand simulation, Banker won about 50.7% of decided hands after every pattern tested.
  • Only the cards left in the shoe move the numbers, and usually by a fraction of a percent.
